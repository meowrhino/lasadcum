laSADCUM (Barcelona, 2019) is the dance project of Barcelona choreographer and dancer Guillem Jiménez, and the home of much of Guillem’s artistic practice.

Its work starts from an analysis of the cultural dynamics of post-internet society, understanding digital culture not only as a context but also as a trait of identity and a territory for choreographic research.

Through stage creation, laSADCUM addresses, from dance, questions related to technology, mechanisms of control, capitalism and the logics of consumption, exploring how they run through bodies, behaviours and contemporary forms of subjectivity. Its stage works operate as choreographic fictions from which to explore the contradictions, affects, corporealities, tensions and fissures of digimodernism, bringing into friction certain complex emotions that shape contemporary experience.

Founded in Barcelona, laSADCUM has received several awards and its work has been shown in various places across Europe, working both locally and internationally.

laSADCUM is an associate artist of the Mercat de les Flors 2026-2029.
