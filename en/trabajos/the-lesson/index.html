THE LESSON (2020)

dance piece / laSADCUM

Through mechanisms of appropriation and collage, THE LESSON works as a choreographic 'found footage' that thinks about our behavior in the post-internet era.

  • Award ACT AWARD 2021 to best international short dance piece
  • Award of the Jury to best dance piece at the Festival 10 Sentidos 2021
Direction and coreography
Guillem Jiménez
Performers
Guillem Jiménez and Vera Palomino
Coproduction
Maricas Marcias / TKM ROOM

CLASSMATES (2020)

exibition / laSADCUM x TKM ROOM

CLASSMATES is an exhibition/event created for TKM Room (Barcelona). The exhibition is about the union between education, copy and saturation. The exhibition features pre-existing works (installation from DANCE LESSONS and the piece THE LESSON) and new works (#FACEAPPropriation, the pre-performance of THE LESSON and NintendoSelfie).

Concept and construction
Guillem Jiménez
Performers
Guillem Jiménez & Vera Palomino
Coproduction
