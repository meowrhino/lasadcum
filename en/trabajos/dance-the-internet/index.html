DANCE THE INTERNET (2022)

workshop / laSADCUM

DANCE THE INTERNET is a workshop around the ACLUCALLS piece where different bodies in movement are explored, based on concepts such as workout, screen and computer programming.

A body session that provides physical tools to all those who want to move and to discover (and test) how the body is affected by the virtuality of the network (full of images in movement of bodies, many movements of many different bodies) based on improvisation guidelines, the imaginary itself and also physical limitations.
