THE LESSON (2020)

dance piece / laSADCUM

Through mechanisms of appropriation and collage, THE LESSON works as a choreographic 'found footage' that thinks about our behavior in the post-internet era.

  • Award ACT AWARD 2021 to best international short dance piece
  • Award of the Jury to best dance piece at the Festival 10 Sentidos 2021
Direction and coreography
Guillem Jiménez
Performers
Guillem Jiménez and Vera Palomino
Coproduction
Maricas Marcias / TKM ROOM
