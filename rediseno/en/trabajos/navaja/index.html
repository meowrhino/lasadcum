NAVAJA (2024)

dance piece / laSADCUM

NAVAJA presents a scenic self-portrait on sex-affective poetics in self-learning dictated by the algorithm in SADCUM adolescence. Five characters called LOINKS embark on a (non)journey framed in an abandoned half-programmed demo.

  • Award to Best Show at DansaCat Awards 2024
  • Award of the Jury to best dance piece at the Festival Pedra Viva 2024
