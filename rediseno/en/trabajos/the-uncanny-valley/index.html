THE UNCANNY VALLEY (2019)

dance piece / laSADCUM

Ulay and Eva are two fictional characters who live in a virtual reality video game. Framed in on Baroque-futurist landscape, Ulay and Eva coexist in a dystopic reflection of the virtualization of the human body, they show the denaturation of the humber and the mechanization of its actions.
