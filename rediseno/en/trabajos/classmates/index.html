CLASSMATES (2020)

exibition / laSADCUM x TKM ROOM

CLASSMATES is an exhibition/event created for TKM Room (Barcelona). The exhibition is about the union between education, copy and saturation. The exhibition features pre-existing works (installation from DANCE LESSONS and the piece THE LESSON) and new works (#FACEAPPropriation, the pre-performance of THE LESSON and NintendoSelfie).
