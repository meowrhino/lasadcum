THE MASKED BODY (2022)

workshop programme / laSADCUM x CCCB

In connection with the exhibition The Mask Never Lies (CCCB), we proposed a detailed programme on the relationship between the body and technology to reflect on the shapes of the contemporary (in)visibility of the body.

The curator of activities had ‘DOMESTIC FICTIONS’ workshop on virtualized household appliances, with Casa Antillón, ‘PRESENTING THE BODY ON THE VIRUAL STAGE’ Workshop about performance in the social media, with Candela Capitnn, ‘CAMOUFLAGING THE BODY FROM SURVILLANCE’ workshop on facial recognition and identification, with Mentah and ‘DANCE THE INTERNET’ workshop on movement and the body using digital foun footage, with Guillem Jiménez; as well as the DJsets of JOVENDELAPERLA b2b BERENICE and DA ROCHA.
