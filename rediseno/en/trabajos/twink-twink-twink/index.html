TWINK TWINK TWINK (2022)

exhibition / laSADCUM

‘TWINK TWINK TWINK’ is a journey through the virtual learning of a generation of saturated teenagers, to the sound of Stravinsky’s emblematic work ‘Le Sacre du Printemps’, which so many choreographers have performed. The sketch of a project on the bodies generated by virtual learning from a choreographic and pornographic perspective; the moment of transition from child to man as a fetish that, like the Sacre, returns time and time again.

Sacred was made and I danced again. I learned to dance through what I saw, to speak in English, to dress and to fuck. But I learned to see this, I learned, I don't know what anymore. My teenager's crush was a 40-man who penetrated one of 18.

The influence of body images on my body: they sculpt it, modulate it and program it. Another Sacre was made: a boy in uniform, uniform body, a boy transiting man, my teenage crush that so many got in. Drink of all the images I consumed enclosed in the bathroom, building my algorithm. Child who mutates man: Sacre returns.

On my 18th birthday, I was alone in Shanghai, and I just got a SAD CUM alone in bed.
